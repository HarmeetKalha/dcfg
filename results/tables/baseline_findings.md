# Baseline robustness findings (frozen detectors, FF++ c23 test; source results/raw/base_*.json)
1. The problem exists on our setup. All 4 detectors drop from clean 0.970-0.981 frame AUC; every severe condition is far outside the clean CI. Mild conditions (JPEG q90/q70, blur s1, resize 0.5) cost <=0.02, except EfficientNet-B4 on noise s5 (-0.087).
2. Hurt most: noise s>=15 and blur s4 (frame AUC 0.53-0.65, close to chance; Xception noise s20 0.541 [0.502, 0.581], EffB4 blur s4 0.533 [0.483, 0.581]), then JPEG q10 (0.71-0.78) and mixed_heavy (0.68-0.73). JPEG q30, blur s2, resize 0.25 are ~-0.05 to -0.08.
3. Detectors differ little except on noise: F3Net is more noise-robust (s10 0.831 [0.800, 0.860] vs Xception 0.703 [0.659, 0.743], CIs disjoint); on blur s3 EffB4 is worst (0.670). Elsewhere CIs overlap heavily; no detector is uniformly robust.
4. Held-out types (never trained on, later): WebP q50 0.888-0.898, q20 0.831-0.846, motion k7 0.913-0.946, k11 0.828-0.862. WebP q50 costs ~0.083-0.088, about 2.5x the drop of JPEG q50 (-0.026 to -0.036).
5. c40 (real H.264, 22,340 frames / 700 videos): frame AUC Xception 0.8046, EffB4 0.7903, F3Net 0.8044, UCF 0.8004 (drop 0.17-0.18; video AUC 0.853-0.868). All four CIs overlap; the drop is smaller than JPEG q10 (-0.20 to -0.27) but larger than any mild synthetic condition.
6. Freq study (2000 crops, descriptive only): blur and JPEG remove mid/high-frequency Y energy (blur s4 radial 4-6: -79%; q10: -87%; chroma high bins -23% for blur but -67% for q10), while noise adds energy everywhere (Y 7-9 bins +26x at s20, chroma ~+54x). The severe drops align with the largest energy changes in this ordering only loosely.
7. Caution: energy change alone does not predict the drop. blur s1 and resize 0.25 have similar Y mean change (-0.54 vs -0.53) but drops of 0.006-0.013 vs 0.05-0.07. WebP leaves Y energy almost unchanged (about 0 to -9%) yet costs 0.08-0.14, so the damage is not captured by this energy statistic (likely phase/structure or artifacts it does not measure). Noise s5 (+2.6x to +8x energy) hurts less than blur s2 for most detectors.
8. Caveats: CIs are video-level bootstraps per condition (1000 resamples); drops are differences of point estimates without paired CIs; the DeepfakeBench weights beat the published clean AUCs by 0.011-0.015 (already accepted at GATE 4). Nothing here says whether a front-end will help; it only shows headroom (best case: recover toward 0.97).
